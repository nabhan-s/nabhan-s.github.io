Two sides, one outcome

Every project creates opportunity for students and capacity for the organizations they serve.

For students

Mentorship that opens doors

Mentorship from experienced engineers, designers, and product leaders shapes job opportunities and career growth.

Real experience, earlier

Working with real nonprofits on production systems builds the kind of experience employers look for, especially for younger students who are still searching for their first opportunity.

For nonprofits and the world

Data into decisions

Nonprofits already hold a wealth of data. Analytics turns it into insight that guides programs, funding, and outreach.

Time back for the mission

Web apps, mobile apps, and AI tools cut administrative work and free up staff time, so more of it goes to the people they serve.

Students gain skills and careers. Organizations gain capacity and reach.Together, that is how the whole community grows.
