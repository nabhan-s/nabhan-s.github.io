Understand the goal

Growth should
open doors.

SDG 8 is one of the UN’s 17 Sustainable Development Goals. It calls for inclusive, sustainable economic growth and decent work for everyone.

That means fair working conditions, opportunities to develop skills, and growth that uses resources responsibly. Two relevant targets focus on innovation and productivity (8.2) and productive employment and decent work (8.5).

Sustainability Next

Georgia Tech’s Sustainability Next plan connects education, research, and campus operations with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

We see Bits of Good’s combination of student learning and nonprofit service as a practical way to contribute to that broader vision: develop skills, understand community needs, and apply technology with purpose.

Technology alone does not guarantee decent work or economic growth. Its value depends on who can access it and whether it addresses a real need.
