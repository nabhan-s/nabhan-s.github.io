UN Sustainable Development Goal 8

Decent Work and Economic Growth

Building a more inclusive economy through opportunity, practical experience, and technology that serves communities.

What is SDG 8?

The United Nations established 17 Sustainable Development Goals to address global challenges. SDG 8 focuses on promoting sustained, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth, productive employment, and decent work for all.

Decent work

People deserve access to productive employment, fair working conditions, and opportunities to develop their skills and build sustainable careers.

Economic opportunity

Inclusive growth means expanding access to opportunity so that individuals and communities can participate in and benefit from economic progress.

Sustainable growth

Long-term prosperity depends on productive organizations, effective use of resources, and approaches to growth that can endure over time.

Why does SDG 8 matter?

Opportunity isn't equally accessible

Students and early-career professionals need opportunities to apply their knowledge, gain practical experience, and develop skills that prepare them for employment.

Nonprofits face resource constraints

Community organizations often balance ambitious missions with limited staff, funding, and technical resources. The right tools can help them manage their work more effectively.

How Bits of Good contributes

Bits of Good connects these two needs by bringing Georgia Tech students together with Atlanta nonprofits to build software that supports their missions.

Students gain experience

Students collaborate on real software projects, practice technical problem-solving, and learn to work with teammates and nonprofit partners. These experiences help prepare them for future careers.

Nonprofits gain capacity

Student teams develop software tailored to partner organizations' needs. Better digital tools can simplify administrative tasks, improve access to information, and help organizations direct resources toward their missions.

Communities benefit

When students develop practical skills and nonprofits strengthen their operations, the community gains from both workforce development and more capable local organizations.

From technology to impact

Technology alone does not guarantee decent work or economic growth. Its impact depends on how it is designed, who can access it, and whether it addresses a genuine need. By pairing student learning with nonprofit partnerships, Bits of Good offers one practical way to connect technical skills with community benefit.
